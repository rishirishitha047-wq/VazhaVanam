# Strands SDK: Workshop to Ship: official rules (summary)

Source: the hackathon page on AWS Builder Center (Build > Hackathons > Strands SDK: Workshop to Ship), copied 26 Oct 2026. If anything here disagrees with the live page, the live page wins.

## Window
- 28 Oct 2026 12:00 AM PDT to 4 Nov 2026 11:59 PM PST. In IST: 28 Oct 12:30 PM to 5 Nov 1:29 PM.
- Gate 1 (AI and human scoring): week of 9 Nov. Gate 2 (human judging): week of 16 Nov. Winners: week of 23 Nov.

## To qualify
- 18 or older with a Builder Center profile. Country and employee exclusions are on the Rules tab.
- Complete the free sandbox workshop "Building AI Agents with Strands SDK: From Simple Loops to Multi-Agent Swarms" on Builder Center **inside the window** (Workshops > request free sandbox environment). Required.

## What to submit
1. An agent built with the **Strands Agents SDK** that addresses a real social-good challenge, with measurable improvement for underserved populations, in one area: **education, health or climate resilience**.
2. A project **article on Builder Center**: the agent, what it does, the social-good problem.
3. A link to a **public repository** with the source code.
4. A short **public demo video under 3 minutes** showing the agent working.
5. An **original** agent, not published before.

## How it is judged (25% each)
- **Social impact**: how clearly it delivers measurable improvement for underserved populations; depth of benefit.
- **Technical execution**: how well it is built with the Strands Agents SDK and how well it works.
- **Communication quality**: how clearly the article and video explain the agent, the problem and what you learned; makes people want to try it.
- **Originality**: originality of the agent architecture, novel use of AWS services, and how far it goes beyond the workshop lessons.

Gate 1 scores every qualifying submission (AI and human); the top 50 go to Gate 2, an AWS expert panel.

## Prizes
Three winners, each $5,000 AWS Promotional Credits + an AWS Builder swag bundle (about $600) + a winner's certificate. AWS Social Impact Credits are a separate programme; nothing in the hackathon depends on it.
